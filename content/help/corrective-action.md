## What this feature is

Corrective Action records the chosen permanent fixes, responsible owners, dates, expected results, and review notes.

## Why it matters

A customer-ready 8D must show why each action addresses a verified cause, not just that someone completed a task.

## When to use it

Use it after D4 root cause is credible and before implementation validation in D6.

## Step-by-step operation

1. Open D5 Corrective Action.
2. Map each proposed action to a D4 occurrence or escape cause.
3. Assign owner, due date, and expected result.
4. Add evidence or approval notes where needed.
5. Do not close D5 until the action is specific enough to validate.

## Example content

Update fixture cleaning sign-off and outgoing adhesion checklist because D4 identified missed cleaning verification and a detection gap.

## Common mistakes

- Adding generic retraining without changing the process or control.
- Choosing actions before root cause is verified.
- Leaving due dates or owners blank.
