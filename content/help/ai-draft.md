## What this feature is

AI Draft helps turn complaint emails, inspection notes, photo descriptions, and 5-Why notes into draft report fields. It does not create evidence or approve the report.

## Why it matters

Drafting can save typing time, but an 8D report must still be owned by engineering judgment. The feature is intentionally positioned as an assistant.

## When to use it

Use it after you have source material but before the report fields are complete. It is not a substitute for investigation, test data, or approval.

## Step-by-step operation

1. Open the AI dialog from a report.
2. Paste only relevant complaint notes, inspection facts, and investigation notes.
3. Choose Generate draft.
4. Review the draft preview carefully.
5. Apply only to empty fields, then edit the wording to match verified evidence.

## Example content

Pasted material says 18 of 500 brackets failed visual coating inspection. The draft may suggest D2 wording, but the engineer must verify the count and scope before keeping it.

## Common mistakes

- Pasting sensitive unrelated data.
- Applying draft text without checking evidence.
- Treating draft wording as customer approval or root-cause verification.
