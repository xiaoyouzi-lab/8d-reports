## What this feature is

AI Quality Check is a conservative review assistant. It can flag missing information, root-cause weaknesses, corrective-action issues, and customer rejection risks.

## Why it matters

A second pass helps catch gaps before export, but final approval must remain with human reviewers and actual evidence.

## When to use it

Use it after key D0-D8 fields are filled and before internal review, export, or customer submission.

## Step-by-step operation

1. Open the AI dialog from the report.
2. Choose Review report.
3. Read readiness, score, critical issues, missing evidence, and suggestions.
4. Update the report only when the suggestion is supported by actual evidence.
5. If the tool says context is missing, record No relevant data rather than inventing findings.

The screenshot above shows where this feature appears in the report workflow. Use it to match the guidance with the product screen. If your screen looks different, check your plan, role, report status, or workspace permissions.

## Example content

If D6 says action complete but has no sample size, AI Quality Check may flag validation risk. The team should add test results or state that validation evidence is not yet available.

## Common mistakes

- Calling AI output approval.
- Adding fabricated test results to satisfy a finding.
- Ignoring locked report restrictions before trying to run AI review.
