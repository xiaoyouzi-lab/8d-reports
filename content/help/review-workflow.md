## What this feature is

The workflow records the report state and helps Team users protect approved, submitted, and closed records from casual edits.

## Why it matters

Review state matters when several people edit a report or when a customer asks which revision was delivered.

## When to use it

Use Draft during writing, Internal Review before approval, Approved before delivery, Submitted after customer delivery, and Closed after final completion.

## Step-by-step operation

1. Open the report workflow panel.
2. Review missing content and knowledge readiness warnings.
3. Move Draft to Internal Review when the report is ready for reviewers.
4. Only owners should move reports to Approved, Submitted, or Closed.
5. Recognize that approved, submitted, and closed states lock the report.

## Example content

A Team owner moves a completed Rev.0 from Internal Review to Approved. The report becomes locked before PDF, Word, or Excel export is sent.

## Common mistakes

- Using Approved before evidence review.
- Changing a locked report without recording a revision reason.
- Treating status as customer acceptance unless the customer actually accepted the report.
