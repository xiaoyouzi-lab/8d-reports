## What this feature is

Preventive Action turns one fix into a system improvement. It captures control-plan changes, work instruction updates, audit changes, and deployment to similar areas.

## Why it matters

Customers often look for evidence that the issue will not return in the same or related process.

## When to use it

Use it after corrective actions are implemented and validation evidence shows the fix works.

## Step-by-step operation

1. Open D7 Prevent Recurrence.
2. Identify similar products, lines, suppliers, or processes that need the lesson.
3. Record document, training, audit, PFMEA, or control-plan updates.
4. Assign owners for deployment.
5. Attach revised documents or audit evidence when available.

After coating fixture cleaning was corrected, the team updated the line-change checklist and layered audit for all coating lines.

## Common mistakes

- Repeating the D5 action without system deployment.
- Claiming recurrence prevention without document or control change.
- Forgetting similar process review.
