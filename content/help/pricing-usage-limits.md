## What this feature is

Pricing and usage limits explain which plan supports report creation, watermark behavior, Word and Excel export, editable sharing, Team roles, and workflow controls.

## Why it matters

Clear limits prevent surprise during export or collaboration, especially when a customer deadline is close.

## When to use it

Check this before relying on formal export, multiple reports, Team workflow, or editable collaboration.

## Step-by-step operation

1. Review the Pricing page.
2. Use Free for evaluation and limited reports.
3. Use Pro for regular individual report delivery.
4. Use Team for shared workspace, roles, workflow, locking, revisions, and activity history.
5. Use single-report export when only one selected report needs formal export.

## Example content

Free users can evaluate with limited reports and watermarked PDF. Pro, Team, or single export unlock no-watermark PDF, Word, and Excel for eligible reports.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming Free includes unlimited formal export.
- Using single export when a team needs ongoing shared workflow.
- Confusing usage limits with data loss; existing reports remain accessible.
