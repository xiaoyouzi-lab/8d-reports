## What this feature is

Fishbone fields help organize possible causes across people, equipment, material, method, measurement, and environment.

## Why it matters

A fishbone review reduces the chance that the team jumps to the first plausible cause and misses a competing process or detection factor.

## When to use it

Use it for complex, recurring, or cross-functional issues where one linear 5-Why chain may be too narrow.

## Step-by-step operation

1. Open D4 Root Cause.
2. Fill the relevant 6M fishbone prompts.
3. Mark unsupported branches as unverified instead of presenting them as facts.
4. Use evidence to narrow the list to verified occurrence and escape causes.
5. Attach photos, check sheets, or analysis files if the team used a separate whiteboard or worksheet.

## Example content

Material: coating batch within spec. Method: fixture cleaning sign-off skipped. Measurement: outgoing checklist did not include edge adhesion.

## Common mistakes

- Listing every imaginable cause without evidence.
- Leaving fishbone branches in the final report as if all were root causes.
- Skipping detection or escape analysis.
