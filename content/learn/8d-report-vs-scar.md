## The relationship

SCAR is the request or case from a customer or buyer asking a supplier to respond. 8D is one common structure used to prepare that response.

## What a SCAR usually asks for

A SCAR may ask for containment, root cause, corrective action, validation, due dates, evidence, and final approval.

## Where 8D Reports helps

The product gives the supplier a guided D0-D8 workspace and export path for the response package.

## What it does not do

It does not automatically submit to customer portals or bypass customer review. Final submission remains manual.
