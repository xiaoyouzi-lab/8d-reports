# Revenue Lead Follow-Up Templates

## Purpose

These templates help respond consistently to Template Setup, Team Launch, and
Assisted First 8D / SCAR Delivery leads. They are for manual email or chat use
only. Do not connect them to live email sending in this PR.

These templates are for manual email or chat use only.

Use a professional, practical tone for English-speaking manufacturing quality
teams. Do not promise guaranteed customer acceptance, certified approval,
instant turnaround, unlimited free consulting, or outcomes that depend on
missing evidence or customer review.

## Shared Rules

- Keep replies short and specific.
- Ask for one concrete next step.
- Confirm what the user needs to provide.
- Explain deliverables plainly.
- Avoid fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake customer stories, and unsupported
  claims.
- Do not include private customer, supplier, product, batch, root cause,
  corrective action, lessons learned, attachment content, payment details, or
  credentials in tracking notes.
- Keep quotes and paid scope human-reviewed before sending.

## Manual Tracking

Track only safe fields:

- request type
- reply sent yes/no
- reply date
- next step selected
- quote amount if sent
- paid service conversation yes/no
- won/lost/pending
- non-sensitive reason category

Do not track full message bodies, customer names, supplier names, product names,
batch numbers, root cause text, corrective action text, lessons learned,
attachment content, credentials, or payment details in analytics.
