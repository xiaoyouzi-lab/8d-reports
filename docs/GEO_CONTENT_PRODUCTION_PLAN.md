# GEO Content Production Plan

## Purpose

This plan turns the revenue query map into a 30-day content production schedule
for 8D Reports. It is designed to prioritize revenue-near, high-intent content
without publishing thin SEO pages, fake statistics, fabricated customer stories,
generic AI fluff, or AI-written filler.

All topics are planning hypotheses until validated by first-party analytics,
GSC, GA4, demo downloads, service CTA clicks, lead submits, signup, export
attempts, Knowledge Base usage, or assisted service conversations.

## GEO Writing Rules

Every article must:

- Answer the target question in the first 80 words.
- Include a practical checklist.
- Use manufacturing, SQE, supplier quality, customer complaint, containment,
  root cause, corrective action, validation, prevention, and closure vocabulary
  naturally.
- Include at least one example table, comparison table, or checklist table.
- Include a `Common mistakes` section.
- Include `When to use Template Setup / Assisted First 8D`.
- Link to the most relevant demo, sample report, service page, pricing page, or
  Knowledge Base page.
- Avoid fake statistics, fake customer stories, fake customer logos, generic AI
  fluff, keyword stuffing, guaranteed acceptance claims, certification claims,
  and unsupported full-QMS claims.
- Keep AI positioning conservative: AI can help review gaps, but it does not
  approve, certify, submit, or invent evidence.

## Answer-First Structure

Use this default structure unless a topic needs a better reader flow:

1. Direct answer in the first 80 words.
2. Who the article is for.
3. Practical checklist.
4. Example table.
5. Common mistakes.
6. When to use Template Setup / Assisted First 8D.
7. Relevant demo/sample/internal links.
8. Soft CTA.
9. FAQ or short answer block when useful.

## Platform Repurposing

- Do not auto-post.
- Do not spam.
- Do not fabricate personal experience.
- Do not over-link.
- Repurposed content should be platform-native and manually reviewed.

### LinkedIn Post Version

- Use for service, Team Launch, quality manager, and SQE workflow topics.
- Hook: one practical manufacturing-quality pain point.
- Short story/problem: describe a generic workflow issue without pretending it
  happened to a real customer.
- Practical takeaway: one checklist, table, or decision rule.
- Soft CTA: invite readers to download a demo report, compare their template, or
  review their first 8D workflow.
- Link suggestion: one natural link at the end, not multiple links.

### Medium Article Version

- Use for comparison, AI, Knowledge Base, and operating-system topics.
- Write in an editorial/analysis style instead of duplicating onsite copy.
- Expand the decision framework and link naturally to 8D Reports once when it
  helps the reader.
- Do not claim independent benchmarks, rankings, or usage numbers unless real
  evidence exists.

### Quora Answer Version

- Answer the question directly in the first paragraph.
- Include one concise checklist or table.
- Mention 8D Reports only when relevant and disclose product context honestly.
- Include at most one natural link.
- Avoid repeating the same link across many answers.

### Reddit-Safe Discussion Version

- Ask a real practitioner question or share a neutral checklist for feedback.
- No sales pitch.
- No link unless the subreddit context clearly allows it.
- Target subreddit names only as suggestions, not posting instructions.
- Do not imply personal experience or customer examples that did not happen.

## Measurement Rules

- Use safe events such as `seo_page_view`, `marketing_cta_clicked`,
  `pricing_service_cta_clicked`, `demo_report_downloaded`,
  `knowledge_search_used`, and `ai_report_review_clicked`.
- Safe metadata can include page id, category, CTA, service, format, source,
  priority, and plan.
- Forbidden analytics metadata includes full user queries, customer names, supplier names, product names, report text, root cause text, corrective action text, lessons learned, batch or lot numbers, uploaded file content, AI prompts, and raw AI output.
- Do not collect full user queries, customer names, supplier names, product
  names, report text, root cause text, corrective action text, lessons learned,
  batch or lot numbers, uploaded file content, AI prompts, or raw AI output.
- Interpret early results as directional evidence, not proof of product-market
  fit.
